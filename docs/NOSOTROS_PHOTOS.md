# LA MIGA — Nuestra Historia photography

Six original, generated editorial images produced specifically for the fictional LA MIGA bakery brand. They do not document an actual physical location or existing production.

| Filename | Subject and role |
| --- | --- |
| `fachada.webp` | Concept storefront image alongside the page introduction |
| `hogaza.webp` | Atmospheric artisan loaf alongside the origin story |
| `miga.webp` | Wide crumb-detail image with a short editorial caption |
| `trigo.webp` | Wheat detail for the simplicity value |
| `harina.webp` | Flour and scoop for craftsmanship |
| `obrador.webp` | Small bakery interior for neighborhood identity |

Each photo is stored in `public/assets/nosotros/`, optimized to WebP and linked to the existing PhotoSwipe lightbox. Images use native width/height declarations and lazy loading below the introduction.

This series is warmer and more narrative than the isolated store product cut-outs, and concentrates on the bakery's identity rather than duplicating the equipment sequence on `/obrador/`. The original approved logo remains unchanged in the shared navbar.
